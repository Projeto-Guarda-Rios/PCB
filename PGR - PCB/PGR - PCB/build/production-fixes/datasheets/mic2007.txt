       Fixed and Adjustable Current Limiting Power Distribution Switches

Features                                            General Description
MIC20X3 - MIC20X9                                   MIC20XX family of switches are current limiting,
  - 70 mΩ Typical On-Resistance @ 5V                high-side power switches, designed for general
                                                    purpose power distribution and control in digital
MIC2005A/20X9A                                      televisions (DTV), printers, set top boxes (STB), PCs,
  - 170 mΩ Typical On-Resistance @ 5V               PDAs, and other peripheral devices (see MIC20XX
• Enable Active-High or Active-Low                  Family Package Types and Table 0-1).
• 2.5V - 5.5V Operating Range                       MIC20XX family’s primary functions are current limiting
• Pre-Set Current Limit Values of 0.5A, 0.8A, and   and power switching. They are thermally protected and
  1.2A*                                             will shutdown should their internal temperature reach
                                                    unsafe levels, protecting both the device and the load,
• Adjustable Current Limit 0.2A to 2.0A*
                                                    under high-current or fault conditions.
  (MIC20X7-MIC20X9)
• Adjustable Current Limit 0.1A to 0.9A*            Features include fault reporting, fault blanking to
  (MIC20X9A)                                        eliminate noise-induced false alarms, output slew rate
                                                    limiting, under voltage detection, automatic-on output,
• Undervoltage Lockout (UVLO)
                                                    and enable pin with choice of either active low or active
• Variable UVLO Allows Adjustable UVLO              high enable. The FET is self-contained, with a fixed- or
  Thresholds*                                       user-adjustable current limit. The MIC20XX family is
• Automatic Load Discharge for Capacitive Loads*    ideal for any system where current limiting and power
• Soft-Start Prevents Large Current Inrush          control are desired.
• Adjustable Slew Rate Allows Custom Slew Rates*    The MIC201X (3 ≤ X ≤ 9) and MIC2019A switches offer
• Automatic-On Output After Fault                   a unique new patented feature: Kickstart which allows
• Thermal Protection                                momentary high-current surges up to the secondary
                                                    current limit (ILIMIT_2nd) without sacrificing overall
* Available on some family members
                                                    system safety.

Applications                                        The MIC20XX family is offered, depending on the
                                                    desired features, in a space-saving 5-pin SOT-23, 6-pin
• Digital Televisions (DTV)                         SOT-23, and 2 mm x 2 mm DFN packages.
• Set Top Boxes
• PDAs
• Printers
• USB / IEEE 1394 Power Distribution
• Desktop and Laptop PCs
• Game Consoles
• Docking Stations

4.10     Thermal Shutdown
Thermal shutdown is employed to protect the MIC20XX
family of switches from damage should the die
temperature exceed safe operating levels. Thermal
shutdown shuts off the output MOSFET and asserts the
FAULT/ output if the die temperature reaches 145°C.
The switch will automatically resume operation when
the die temperature cools down to 135°C. If resumed
operation results in reheating of the die, another
shutdown cycle will occur and the switch will continue
cycling between ON and OFF states until the
overcurrent condition has been resolved.
Depending on PCB layout, package type, ambient
temperature, etc., hundreds of milliseconds may
elapse from the incidence of a fault to the output
MOSFET being shut off. This delay is due to thermal
time constants within the system itself. In no event will
the device be damaged due to thermal overload
because die temperature is monitored continuously by
on-chip circuitry.
